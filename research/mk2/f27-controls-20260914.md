# F27 deterministic controls — 14 September 2026

This package adds 20 prospective battles and one separately labeled context-discovery pilot. All use non-FC T5 Infantry and Marksmen without heroes. Ten prospective fights use 100 Infantry versus 100 Marksmen; ten use 100 Infantry versus 101 Marksmen. Each pairing was fought five times in each direction.

The unchanged locked kernel matches the winner and all six survivor slots in all 21 reports. Chance-skill counters are absent, not observed zero. The optional casualty calculator is not validated by these comparisons; raw injury categories are preserved as evidence.

Frozen alternatives on the 20 prospective reports: original/source-ceiling/locked/historical-calibrated 20/20, fractional participation 10/20, floor participation 0/20. These results reject those particular replacements in this context. They do not explain the earlier FC1 mismatches or establish a universal rule.

Recorded packet seeds and independently captured battle timestamps are preserved separately. Eight prospective cases have seed equal to timestamp; the remaining twelve have seed equal to timestamp plus one. The pilot has the latter relationship. The loader correctly reports the eight disagreements with its timestamp-plus-one assumption and uses the recorded seed.

The sanitized report file contains the decoder's normalized result_data projection. Formation identifiers are anonymized; complete original packet files and encoded report bytes remain in the private evidence archive. Other wire sections, including overview, are not included in that projection. Winner evidence in these fixtures comes from independently checked native report screenshots. Separate research found a raw overview.result field; the absence claim applies only to the normalized projection.

All 21 fixtures were exercised through the actual locked testcase loader. On the bounded published606 plus these21 corpus, results are 385/627 exact, with every prior606 comparison and replay metadata unchanged. This is a bounded corpus count and excludes newer reports collected by the separate calibrated task. No kernel, troop-stat, probability or RNG changes are included.
